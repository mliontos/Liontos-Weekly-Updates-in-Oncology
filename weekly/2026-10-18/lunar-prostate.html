LUNAR · Oligorecurrent Prostate Cancer

European Urology · 12 Oct 2026
GU Oncology · Randomized Phase II

177Lu-PNT2002 before SBRT

PSMA PET/CT-detected oligorecurrent hormone-sensitive prostate cancer with 1–5 lesions amenable to SBRT.

Design and efficacy

N=92: SBRT alone (n=47) versus two cycles of neoadjuvant 177Lu-PNT2002 followed by SBRT (n=45). At median follow-up 31.9 months, median PFS was 17.6 vs 7.4 months; HR 0.38 (95% CI 0.23–0.61; p<0.001). Hormonal therapy-free survival also favored the experimental strategy: HR 0.57 (95% CI 0.34–0.95; p=0.03).

PRACTICE IMPACT · IMPORTANT RANDOMIZED PHASE II; PHASE III VALIDATION NEEDED

Critical appraisal

The magnitude and durability of the PFS effect support further study of radioligand therapy integrated with metastasis-directed SBRT. The trial remains phase II; several disease-trajectory endpoints were exploratory and should not be treated as definitive evidence of cure or OS benefit.
