RT CHARM · Breast Cancer

JCO · 12 Oct 2026
Breast Oncology · Randomized noninferiority trial

Hypofractionated PMRT with breast reconstruction

50 Gy/25 fractions over 5 weeks versus 42.56 Gy/16 fractions over 3 weeks in women undergoing breast reconstruction.

Results

898 patients enrolled; 825 evaluable for the primary endpoint. At 24 months, reconstruction complications were 12.3% with hypofractionated PMRT versus 10.2% with conventional PMRT; estimated difference −2.1% (95% CI −6.5 to 2.2), meeting noninferiority. Thirty-six-month locoregional recurrence was 1.5% versus 2.2%, respectively.

PRACTICE IMPACT · SUPPORTS 16-FRACTION PMRT IN PATIENTS WITH RECONSTRUCTION
