Neoadjuvant SABR · RCC with IVC thrombus

JAMA Oncology · 15 Oct 2026
Kidney cancer · Phase II nonrandomized

SABR before nephrectomy and IVC thrombectomy

Prospective evaluation of neoadjuvant stereotactic ablative radiotherapy in RCC with inferior vena cava tumor thrombus.

Interpretation

Neoadjuvant SABR appeared feasible with an acceptable safety profile. Recurrence-free survival met the prespecified phase II screening criterion, but remains exploratory. Without a randomized comparator, the study cannot establish improvement in oncologic outcomes.
