NeoVax · Ovarian Cancer

Nature Medicine · 14 Oct 2026
Gynecologic Oncology · Phase I

Personalized neoantigen-targeting vaccine

Personalized synthetic long-peptide neoantigen vaccine with poly-ICLC, with delayed anti-PD-1 therapy, studied after platinum chemotherapy in high-grade epithelial ovarian cancer.

EARLY PHASE · Feasibility / safety / immunogenicity signal, not practice changing

Study design

The phase I program evaluated feasibility and safety in newly diagnosed and first-recurrence platinum-sensitive ovarian cancer. Ten patients ultimately received vaccination; the recurrent-disease cohort demonstrated substantially greater manufacturing/vaccination feasibility than the newly diagnosed cohort.

Clinical interpretation

The publication supports biological feasibility of individualized neoantigen vaccination in ovarian cancer and provides translational evidence of vaccine-induced immunity. The sample is very small and the study is not designed to establish comparative efficacy, PFS or OS benefit.
