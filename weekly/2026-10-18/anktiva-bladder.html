ANKTIVA + BCG · NMIBC

FDA · 14 Oct 2026
Bladder cancer · Regulatory

FDA expands indication to papillary-only BCG-unresponsive NMIBC

The FDA expanded nogapendekin alfa inbakicept-pmln (ANKTIVA) with BCG to adults with BCG-unresponsive NMIBC, revising the prior CIS ± papillary indication to include high-grade papillary-only tumors.

PRACTICE CHANGING · US regulatory expansion

Evidence context

The FDA explicitly described this as a new regulatory approach using evidence supporting the earlier CIS approval together with scientific similarities between closely related BCG-unresponsive NMIBC disease states. This distinction is important when interpreting the evidentiary basis for the expanded population.
