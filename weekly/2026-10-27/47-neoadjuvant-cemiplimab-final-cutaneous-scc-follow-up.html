Neoadjuvant cemiplimab | final cutaneous SCC follow-up

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewCross-tumour, melanoma and rare cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Final phase 2 follow-up at a median 54 months: estimated EFS 76.2%, OS 79.4%. There were 16 EFS events overall, with no new disease recurrences; five additional events were comorbidity-related deaths. None of 40 patients with pCR had recurrence. No new grade ≥3 TEAEs occurred.

Clinical interpretation Durable single-arm follow-up is reassuring, but pathologic-response subgroups are not randomised treatment comparisons. Check the full population denominator, CIs and subsequent local/adjuvant therapies before definitive editorial sign-off.

Primary article record: Gross ND et al. J Clin Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1200/JCO-26-02166 | PMID 42921711
