Neoadjuvant cemiplimab | final cutaneous SCC follow-up

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewCross-tumour, melanoma and rare cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

In 79 patients (cutoff 19 November 2025; median follow-up 54 months), estimated 54-month EFS was 76.2% (95% CI 63.2–85.1) and OS 79.4% (67.5–87.3). Sixteen EFS events included three recurrences; none occurred among 40 patients with pCR. Of 70 surgery recipients, 32 underwent observation, 17 radiotherapy and 16 adjuvant cemiplimab; five had unavailable management data.

The journal safety table reports grade ≥3 TEAEs in 15/79 (19.0%), including four grade 5 events; all-cause fatal events are distinct from treatment-related deaths. The poster’s “no new grade ≥3 TEAEs” statement specifically concerns the 16 adjuvant-cemiplimab recipients.

Clinical interpretation Durable single-arm activity; response subgroups do not establish comparative efficacy.

Verified against ESMO 2490P and Gross et al., JCO; DOI 10.1200/JCO-26-02166, including accessible efficacy and safety tables. External supplements were not fully reviewed.
