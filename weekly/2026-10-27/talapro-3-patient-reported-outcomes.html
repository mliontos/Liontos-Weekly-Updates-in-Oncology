TALAPRO-3 | patient-reported outcomes

23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewProstate cancer
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

At the 18 February 2026 cutoff, 474/599 patients had baseline and post-baseline PRO assessments (240 talazoparib–enzalutamide; 234 placebo–enzalutamide). Prespecified measures included EORTC QLQ-C30/PR25 and BPI-SF. Mean global health/QoL difference was −3.7 points (95% CI −6.5 to −1.0), below the abstract’s five-point clinical-importance threshold; appetite-loss difference was +5.2 (2.5–8.0).

The earlier primary rPFS benefit is background, not a new ESMO result. This PRO abstract does not provide updated severe/fatal adverse-event rates.

Clinical interpretation Comparable deterioration estimates do not prove equivalence or absence of treatment burden. Missing PRO assessments and the appetite-loss signal matter. PROs complement, rather than replace, the haematological safety profile of PARP inhibition.
