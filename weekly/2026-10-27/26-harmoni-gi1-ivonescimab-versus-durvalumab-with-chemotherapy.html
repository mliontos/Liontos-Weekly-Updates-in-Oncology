HARMONi-GI1 | ivonescimab versus durvalumab with chemotherapy

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGastrointestinal cancers
Full Evidence Review
Pending verification

The public sponsor report describes 682 patients in China (340/342); median follow-up 14.3 months. OS: 14.9 versus 12.1 months; HR 0.75 (95% CI 0.61–0.92), one-sided P=0.0031. PFS: 8.4 versus 5.8 months; HR 0.61 (0.51–0.72), one-sided P<0.0001. ORR: 53.8% versus 32.5%. Treatment-emergent serious AEs: 43.4% versus 43.7%; discontinuation of ivonescimab/durvalumab due to TEAEs: 3.5% versus 5.6%.

Clinical interpretation Direct phase 3 comparison against an active chemoimmunotherapy regimen is important. Original LBA8/manuscript, full severe/fatal toxicity and anti-VEGF events remain to be checked. Regional generalisability and indication-level regulatory status require assessment. These are sponsor-reported results, not completed article-level verification.

Source status: Summit/Akeso public results release, 25 October 2026; Zhou J et al., ESMO 2026 LBA8. Source / locator
