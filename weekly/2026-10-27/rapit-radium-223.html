RAPIT | real-world radium-223 final analysis

23–27 October 2026

The single-arm study enrolled 224 patients with bone-metastatic mCRPC. Radium-223-related any-grade AEs occurred in 13.8% and serious AEs in 3.1%. Median OS was 21.93 months (95% CI 16.33–not reached); 68.8% received five or six injections. Most patients (67%) received no further therapy after the last dose.

Clinical interpretation Patients completing more doses must survive and remain fit long enough to do so; completion-associated survival is vulnerable to selection and immortal-time bias. These observational data do not replace randomised efficacy or determine the optimal sequence. Serious AEs must not be relabelled as grade ≥3 AEs.
