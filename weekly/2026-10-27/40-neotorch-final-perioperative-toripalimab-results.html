Neotorch | final perioperative toripalimab results

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewThoracic cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Phase 3 in China; 501 assigned (250/251), follow-up 48.2 months. EFS: not reached versus 20.2 months; HR 0.52 (95% CI 0.39–0.68), P<0.0001. Major pathological response: 47% versus 9%, P<0.0001. OS HR 0.66 (0.49–0.89), P=0.0072; medians not reached. No new safety signals were reported, without updated numerical rates in the abstract.

Clinical interpretation Adds long-term survival evidence in resectable stage II/III disease. Compare eligibility, perioperative exposure and local availability with existing regimens; avoid cross-trial efficacy rankings.

Primary article record: Li ZM et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.094 | PMID 42933639
