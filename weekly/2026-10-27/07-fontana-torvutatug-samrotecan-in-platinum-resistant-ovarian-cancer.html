FONTANA | torvutatug samrotecan in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer

Full Evidence Review
Early

Torvu-sam (AZD5335) is an FRα-directed ADC with a topoisomerase-1 inhibitor payload. This phase 1/2a, open-label Module 1 analysis included dose escalation (M1A; FRα-unselected, unrestricted prior lines) and expansion (M1B; ≤3 prior lines). Dose optimisation required ≥25% tumour cells with ≥1+ staining; additional expansion enrolled <25%. At 16 March 2026, 223 patients had been treated: 71 M1A, 124 M1B1 and 28 M1B2; median exposure was 7.1 months. The primary objective was safety/tolerability.

High was defined as ≥75% tumour cells staining ≥2+; the abstract lists low as ≥25% staining ≥1+ and ultralow as <25% staining ≥1+. The printed low definition does not explicitly exclude high tumours; these groups are retained as reported, without reconstructing mutually exclusive assay criteria. The response-table denominator is 143, versus 152 patients enrolled in M1B; the reason for this difference is not provided. PFS and DOR were to be presented and are not numerically available in this abstract.

TEAEs led to dose reduction in 26.5% and discontinuation in 8.1%. The abstract does not provide an overall grade 3–4 TEAE rate, fatal-event rate or ILD rate; absence of those figures is not evidence of absence. Exploratory ctDNA molecular responses were observed across doses and FRα subgroups.

Clinical interpretation Responses across the reported FRα spectrum support further development, with clinically relevant gastrointestinal and marrow toxicity. Small, non-comparative cohorts cannot establish equal efficacy across expression groups or superiority to another ADC. Higher exposure was associated with greater and more durable tumour reduction, but dose selection requires efficacy and safety together. The abstract states that phase 3 TREVI-OC-01 (NCT07218809) was recruiting at the time of reporting.
