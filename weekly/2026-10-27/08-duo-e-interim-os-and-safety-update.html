DUO-E | interim OS and safety update

← Weekly overviewGynaecological cancers

DUO-E/GOG-3041/ENGOT-EN10 assesses carboplatin/paclitaxel plus durvalumab, followed by durvalumab with or without olaparib, in advanced/recurrent endometrial cancer. The sponsor confirms presentation of interim OS and safety as LBA37. Original LBA37 statistical tables and the current manuscript have not been retrieved.

Clinical interpretation The update is included for review, with numerical OS and safety claims withheld until the prespecified interim boundaries are checked. A nominal P<0.05 or a CI excluding 1 is insufficient to establish significance when alpha spending requires a stricter threshold. Do not substitute the earlier DUO-E PFS publication for this OS analysis.

Source status: AstraZeneca congress listing; Westin SN et al. ESMO 2026, LBA37; NCT04269200. Source / locator
