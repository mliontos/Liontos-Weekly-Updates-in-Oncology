DUO-E | interim OS and safety update

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGynaecological cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

At the 2 February 2026 cutoff, interim OS maturity was 52%; median follow-up among censored patients was 52 months. The three arms were carboplatin/paclitaxel (CP; n=241), CP plus durvalumab followed by durvalumab (CP+D; n=238), and CP+D followed by durvalumab plus olaparib (CP+D+O; n=239). Median OS was 39.6, 40.7 and 51.1 months, respectively. Versus CP, OS HRs were 0.87 (95% CI 0.68–1.11; P=0.259) for CP+D and 0.77 (0.60–0.98; P=0.037) for CP+D+O. Neither comparison met the prespecified statistical significance criterion according to LBA37.

Exploratory dMMR OS HRs were 0.57 (0.30–1.05) and 0.43 (0.21–0.83); in pMMR disease, 0.95 (0.73–1.24) and 0.85 (0.65–1.11), respectively. These estimates do not establish a subgroup interaction. The abstract provides no numerical updated severe/fatal safety rates. Its CP+D+O OS event row lists 115/239 alongside 48.7%, an internal arithmetic inconsistency; the event count is retained without adopting that percentage.

Clinical interpretation The numerical OS difference does not establish a confirmatory OS benefit. The full SAP and current manuscript are needed to resolve alpha allocation and the table inconsistency.
