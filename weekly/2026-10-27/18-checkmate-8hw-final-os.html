CheckMate 8HW | final OS

23–27 October 2026

Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

839 randomised across treatment lines; efficacy used centrally confirmed MSI-H/dMMR disease. Median follow-up 68 months. Nivolumab/ipilimumab OS HR versus nivolumab across lines: 0.61 (95% CI 0.46–0.82), P=0.0009; versus chemotherapy in first line: 0.37 (0.24–0.57), P<0.0001. First-line doublet versus nivolumab OS HR: 0.66 (0.44–0.99), P=0.0432, which did not meet P<0.006. Grade 3–4 TRAEs: 22% versus 14% for doublet versus nivolumab across lines.

Clinical interpretation Long-term results strengthen the doublet evidence while requiring careful separation of all-line and first-line comparisons. Do not label the first-line OS comparison against nivolumab statistically positive under the trial’s testing plan.

Primary article record: Lonardi S et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.09.009 | PMID 42926106
