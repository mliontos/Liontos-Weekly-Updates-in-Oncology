Temab-A plus bevacizumab | randomised phase 1 signal

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGastrointestinal cancers
Full Evidence Review
Early

83 treated, with 63 on telisotuzumab adizutecan/bevacizumab and 20 on FTD-TPI/bevacizumab; median three previous lines. At 2.0 and 2.4 mg/kg, ORRs were 19% and 30%, versus 0% for control; PFS 5.6 and 6.8 versus 4.2 months; OS 11.2 and 14.7 versus 10.2 months. OS HRs were 0.75 and 0.62, respectively. Reported treatment discontinuations were 3/26 and 1/30 in the dose cohorts.

Clinical interpretation Small, dose-ranging evidence; no confirmatory survival claim. CIs, significance tests and complete severe-toxicity rates were not available in the abstract. A selected 2.4-mg/kg result should not stand in for the full dose population.

Primary article record: Cecchini M et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.105 | PMID 42926107
