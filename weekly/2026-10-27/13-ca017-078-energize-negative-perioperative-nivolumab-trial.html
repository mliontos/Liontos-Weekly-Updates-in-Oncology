CA017-078 / ENERGIZE | negative perioperative nivolumab trial

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGenitourinary cancers
Full Evidence Review
Negative

Phase 3; 653 assigned (325/328); median follow-up 54.5 months. pCR: 39.1% versus 29.6%; adjusted difference 9.5 points, 99.91% CI −2.6 to 21.6. EFS HR 0.94 (95.74% CI 0.71–1.25), P=0.6697. OS descriptive: HR 0.88 (95% CI 0.64–1.21). Grade 3–4 TRAEs: 50.0% versus 49.5%; one treatment-related death with nivolumab. ctDNA clearance: 87/107 (81.3%) versus 66/92 (71.7%).

Clinical interpretation Neither co-primary endpoint established benefit. OS was not formally testable after the EFS result. ctDNA was prognostic; these exploratory results do not validate a ctDNA-guided treatment strategy.

Primary article record: Sonpavde G et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.096 | PMID 42918420
