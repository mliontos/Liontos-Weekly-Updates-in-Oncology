PERSEE | chemoimmunotherapy versus pembrolizumab at PD-L1 ≥50%

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewThoracic cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Phase 3; 321 assigned (160/161), follow-up 38.7 months. PFS: 16.1 months (95% CI 11.0–34.1) versus 6.9 (5.5–9.3); HR 0.56 (0.43–0.74), P<0.001. OS: 50.2 versus 29.9 months; HR 0.77 (0.56–1.05). Grade ≥3 TRAEs: 54% versus 19%; grade ≥3 immune-related AEs: 13% in each group.

Clinical interpretation The direct comparison informs the efficacy/toxicity trade-off in PD-L1-high disease. PFS improved; OS is not statistically established by the reported CI. Do not assume the larger median OS difference proves a survival benefit.

Primary article record: Descourt R et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.108 | PMID 42927854
