Phase 3 in China; 498 assigned (250/248), median follow-up 7.7 months. BICR PFS: 8.3 versus 5.4 months; HR 0.47 (95% CI 0.36–0.60), one-sided P<0.0001. Grade ≥3 TRAEs: 41% (101/248) versus 39% (93/239). Discontinuations for TRAEs: three per arm. One death of unknown cause in the experimental arm was considered possibly treatment related.

Clinical interpretation Another PFS-positive ADC strategy in EGFR-TKI resistance. Short follow-up and immature survival restrict conclusions. Retain the distinction between a possibly related death and confirmed treatment causality.

Primary article record: Xu CR et al. Lancet (2026). DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(26)01899-4 | PMID 42933457
