Krascendo 1 | divarasib versus approved KRAS G12C inhibitors

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewThoracic cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Phase 3; 338 treated (167/171), control predominantly sotorasib (156), with 15 receiving adagrasib. PFS: 9.8 versus 5.7 months; HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.44–0.76), P<0.001. OS: 17.7 versus 12.0 months; HR 0.56 (0.41–0.78), P<0.001. ORR in 328 evaluable patients: 58.4% versus 32.3%; DOR 11.9 versus 6.9 months. Grade 3–4 TRAEs: 24.6% versus 34.5%.

Clinical interpretation Direct randomised evidence of improvement within a targeted class. The pooled comparator is not equally informative about sotorasib and adagrasib because only 15 controls received adagrasib. Regulatory and reimbursement verification remains separate.

Primary article record: Skoulidis F et al. N Engl J Med (2026). DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2605703 | PMID 42929675
