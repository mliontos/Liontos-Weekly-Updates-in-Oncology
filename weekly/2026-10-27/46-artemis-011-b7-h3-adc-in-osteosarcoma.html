ARTEMIS-011 | B7-H3 ADC in osteosarcoma

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewCross-tumour, melanoma and rare cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Phase 3; 122 treated (80/42), median age 21, including 31 adolescents. BICR PFS: 5.5 versus 1.4 months; HR 0.33 (95% CI 0.20–0.55), P<0.0001. Immature OS HR 0.51 (0.21–1.23). Grade ≥3 TRAEs: 93% versus 83%; serious TRAEs: 64% versus 29%; dose reductions: 71% versus 26%. No treatment-related deaths were reported.

Clinical interpretation PFS activity is striking in a high-need population, with a substantial haematological/supportive-care burden. “Manageable” should not substitute for the severe toxicity rates. OS remains uncertain.

Primary article record: Xie L et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.1284 | PMID 42933637
