Phase 3 HER2-positive advanced breast cancer; 885 assigned (445/440). At 21.0 months median follow-up, 24-month PFS: 58.5% versus 34.4% with THP; HR 0.46 (95% CI 0.37–0.56), P<0.001. ORR: 87.6% versus 73.4%. Grade ≥3 AEs: 45.9% (204/444) versus 34.4% (151/439); severe neutropenia 13.1% versus 8.4%. AEs leading to death: two per group.
