IROCAS | adjuvant triplet did not improve DFS

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGastrointestinal cancers
Full Evidence Review
Negative

Phase 3, high-risk stage III colon cancer; 795 assigned, follow-up 55 months. Three-year DFS: 65.6% with mFOLFIRINOX versus 66.3% with mFOLFOX6; HR 1.07 (95% CI 0.84–1.36), P=0.61. Three-year OS: 91.2% versus 86.7%; HR 0.85 (0.61–1.21), P=0.37. Grade ≥3 AEs: 62.2% versus 62.1%; grade ≥3 diarrhoea: 15.6% versus 3.8%, fatigue: 7.3% versus 1.8%.

Clinical interpretation No basis for empirical adjuvant chemotherapy intensification. Similar aggregate severe-AE rates conceal important increases in diarrhoea and fatigue.

Primary article record: Bennouna J et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.104 | PMID 42927853
