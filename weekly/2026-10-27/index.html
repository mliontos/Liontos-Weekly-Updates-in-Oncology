5 things to know

• TroFuse-005 improved PFS and OS after platinum and immunotherapy in endometrial cancer, with more grade ≥3 AEs.

• KEYNOTE-C93 supports a chemotherapy-free strategy in dMMR endometrial cancer against chemotherapy alone; the comparison with chemoimmunotherapy remains unanswered.

• VOLGA and DeLLphi-305 provide important survival evidence in perioperative bladder cancer and maintenance SCLC, respectively.

• Krascendo 1 and SAFFRON strengthen mechanism-selected lung cancer treatment; population and assay definitions matter.

• Negative trials and statistical qualifications change interpretation: non-significant OS, descriptive analyses and failed non-inferiority cannot be presented as confirmatory successes.

Gynaecological cancers

TroFuse-005 | sacituzumab tirumotecan after platinum and immunotherapy

Practice informing

Randomised phase 3; 776 assigned (386/390), with safety populations of 384/367. Median follow-up 14.0 months. PFS: 7.2 months (95% CI 5.6–7.6) versus 3.9 (3.7–5.3); HR 0.59 (0.50–0.70), P<0.0001. OS: 19.0 months (16.1–not reached) versus 12.1 (11.0–15.2); HR 0.68 (0.55–0.84), P=0.0001. Grade ≥3 all-cause AEs: 69% versus 57%; serious AEs: 35% versus 32%; treatment-related deaths: three in each arm. Randomisation incorporated MMR and TROP2 expression.

Clinical interpretation Strong evidence of efficacy in the post-platinum/post-IO setting, with greater severe toxicity. Median differences are 3.3 months for PFS and 6.9 for OS; these are descriptive differences between medians. Do not require a TROP2 threshold without checking the final label. EU authorisation and Greek reimbursement for this indication remain unverified.

Primary article record: Lorusso D et al. Lancet (2026). DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(26)02099-4 | PMID 42933460

KEYNOTE-C93 | first-line pembrolizumab alone in dMMR endometrial cancer

Practice informing

Phase 3, open-label; 299 assigned (149/150). BICR PFS: 38.2 months (95% CI 17.9–not reached) versus 8.4 (6.8–10.4); HR 0.57 (0.42–0.79), P=0.0002. PFS at 6 months: 75% versus 76%; at 12 months: 67% versus 40%; at 36 months: 53% versus 30%. OS did not meet the interim significance criterion: HR 0.77 (0.52–1.15), P=0.10. Grade ≥3 TRAEs: 18% (27/149) versus 51% (73/144); TRAE discontinuations: 9% versus 15%; treatment-related deaths: two versus one.

Clinical interpretation Chemo-free PD-1 therapy is a credible option to investigate in selected dMMR disease. The comparator was chemotherapy alone; superiority to contemporary chemotherapy plus immunotherapy was not tested. The near-identical 6-month PFS rates matter when counselling patients needing early disease control.

Primary article record: Slomovitz BM et al. Lancet (2026). DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(26)02095-7 | PMID 42933458

BEHOLD-1 biomarkers | B7-H4 expression and the tumour microenvironment

Early

This exploratory analysis used screening archival/fresh tissue from BEHOLD-1: B7-H4 IHC in 111 tumours and whole-transcriptome sequencing in 79. Response associations were assessed across all doses at the earlier 12 November 2025 cutoff. Responders had a confirmed response; non-responders included stable/progressive disease and unevaluable patients. The supplied abstract describes this as phase 1b dose expansion, whereas LBA41 describes dose selection; cohort overlap is not resolved.

B7-H4 was expressed in 96% of tumours but was not associated with response. Immune-related pathways were enriched in responders; TNFα–NF-κB signalling had the highest reported enrichment (NES 2.6). Responders had higher macrophage (P=0.02), Treg/Th2 traffic (P=0.0065), myeloid-cell traffic (P=0.049) and matrix-remodelling (P=0.00065) signature scores. Cathepsin B (CTSB) expression was higher in responders (P=0.02). CTSB, macrophage and matrix-remodelling signatures correlated with each other (r=0.5–0.63; P<0.00001).

Clinical interpretation These associations support a hypothesis that a macrophage-rich, matrix-remodelling microenvironment and CTSB facilitate linker cleavage, payload release and bystander activity. This mechanism is a proposed interpretation, not demonstrated causality. High target prevalence did not translate into a response-selection biomarker in this analysis. No validated composite assay, clinical cutoff, independent validation or multiplicity adjustment is supplied; these exploratory signatures should not determine treatment eligibility.

Lheureux S, Salutari V, Oaknin A, et al. BEHOLD-1: exploratory analysis of biomarkers associated with mocertatug rezetecan response in patients with PROC. ESMO Congress 2026; abstract 1248RO. NCT06431594. Sponsor/funder: GSK, study 222730. Abstract text and tables supplied by the editor; figures reconciled against that text. Original congress-page and journal-level verification remain outstanding.

Sofetabart mipitecan plus bevacizumab | platinum-sensitive ovarian cancer

Early

In this phase 1 cohort, 33 patients received the FRα-targeted, exatecan-payload ADC Sofe-M 4 mg/kg plus bevacizumab 15 mg/kg. Eligibility required ECOG 0–1, with no limit on prior therapies and prior mirvetuximab allowed. At the 5 March 2026 cutoff, median age was 67 years (41–83), median prior therapies three (1–6), 70% had prior bevacizumab and 67% prior PARPi.

At median follow-up of six months, reported ORR was 64% (95% CI 45–80): three CRs, 16 PRs and two unconfirmed PRs. This reported ORR therefore must not be labelled a confirmed ORR. DCR was 100% (95% CI 89–100); 18/21 responders (86%) remained on treatment and median DOR was not reached. Numerical PFS was not supplied.

FONTANA | torvutatug samrotecan in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer

Early

Torvu-sam (AZD5335) is an FRα-directed ADC with a topoisomerase-1 inhibitor payload. This phase 1/2a, open-label Module 1 analysis included dose escalation (M1A; FRα-unselected, unrestricted prior lines) and expansion (M1B; ≤3 prior lines). Dose optimisation required ≥25% tumour cells with ≥1+ staining; additional expansion enrolled <25%. At 16 March 2026, 223 patients had been treated: 71 M1A, 124 M1B1 and 28 M1B2; median exposure was 7.1 months. The primary objective was safety/tolerability.

DUO-E | interim OS and safety update

Pending verification

DUO-E/GOG-3041/ENGOT-EN10 assesses carboplatin/paclitaxel plus durvalumab, followed by durvalumab with or without olaparib, in advanced/recurrent endometrial cancer. The sponsor confirms presentation of interim OS and safety as LBA37. Original LBA37 statistical tables and the current manuscript have not been retrieved.

Clinical interpretation The update is included for review, with numerical OS and safety claims withheld until the prespecified interim boundaries are checked. A nominal P<0.05 or a CI excluding 1 is insufficient to establish significance when alpha spending requires a stricter threshold. Do not substitute the earlier DUO-E PFS publication for this OS analysis.

Genitourinary cancers

VOLGA | perioperative durvalumab and enfortumab vedotin

Practice informing

Phase 3 in cisplatin-ineligible/refusing MIBC; durvalumab–EV n=233, durvalumab–tremelimumab–EV n=233, control n=229. Doublet versus control: 24-month EFS 63.5% (95% CI 56.5–69.7) versus 45.1% (38.2–51.8); HR 0.51 (0.38–0.67), P<0.001. OS at 24 months: 78.4% versus 69.4%; HR 0.58 (0.40–0.83), P=0.003. Triplet EFS at 24 months: 60.1%; HR versus control 0.61 (0.47–0.80), P<0.001. Grade ≥3 AEs: 71.1%, 79.5%, 50.2%; grade ≥3 TRAEs in the experimental arms: 30.7%, 44.5%.

Clinical interpretation The doublet produced absolute 24-month EFS and OS gains of 18.4 and 9.0 percentage points. The trial does not establish an advantage for adding tremelimumab or superiority over another active perioperative EV/IO regimen. Assess skin, neuropathy, glucose and immune toxicity in implementation planning.

Primary article record: Powles T et al. N Engl J Med (2026). DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2608323 | PMID 42929692

KEYNOTE-866 | negative EFS result

Negative

Phase 3; 907 assigned. At 60.9 months median follow-up, 60-month EFS was 63.5% versus 61.9%; HR 0.88 (95% CI 0.71–1.10), one-sided P=0.1366. pCR: 44.4% versus 35.8%; model-estimated difference 8.9 points (2.5–15.1). OS HR 1.00 (0.79–1.27). Grade ≥3 TEAEs: 80.2% versus 75.1%.

Clinical interpretation Perioperative pembrolizumab added to neoadjuvant cisplatin/gemcitabine did not improve the primary EFS endpoint. A higher pCR rate does not override the negative survival result. Do not infer a uniform class effect across perioperative checkpoint inhibitors.

Primary article record: Bedke J et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.106 | PMID 42918422

CA017-078 / ENERGIZE | negative perioperative nivolumab trial

Negative

Phase 3; 653 assigned (325/328); median follow-up 54.5 months. pCR: 39.1% versus 29.6%; adjusted difference 9.5 points, 99.91% CI −2.6 to 21.6. EFS HR 0.94 (95.74% CI 0.71–1.25), P=0.6697. OS descriptive: HR 0.88 (95% CI 0.64–1.21). Grade 3–4 TRAEs: 50.0% versus 49.5%; one treatment-related death with nivolumab. ctDNA clearance: 87/107 (81.3%) versus 66/92 (71.7%).

CAPItello-281 | updated descriptive OS

Practice informing

PTEN-deficient de novo mHSPC; 1,012 patients. At an ad hoc cut-off with 42% OS maturity, OS was 57.4 versus 46.5 months; HR 0.82 (95% CI 0.68–0.99), P=0.044. Updated rPFS: 35.5 versus 26.0 months; HR 0.83 (0.70–0.99), nominal P=0.033. The original rPFS analysis had HR 0.81 (0.66–0.98), P=0.034. PTEN deficiency required absent specific cytoplasmic staining in ≥90% of viable malignant cells.

Clinical interpretation The OS update is ad hoc and descriptive, despite a CI excluding 1. Do not present it as a new confirmatory OS success. Updated numerical safety rates require the full article; “no new signals” does not replace those rates. Confirm assay applicability before local adoption.

Primary article record: Fizazi K et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.097 | PMID 42918424

ENZARAD | no overall MFS improvement

Negative

Phase 3; 802 patients receiving radiotherapy and ADT. At 92 months median follow-up, 8-year conventional-imaging MFS was 74% with enzalutamide versus 72% with an older antiandrogen; HR 0.88 (95% CI 0.67–1.15), P=0.34. Grade 3–5 AEs occurred in 185 patients in each arm. A planned pelvic-node radiotherapy subgroup had HR 0.47 (0.29–0.76), adjusted P=0.004.

Clinical interpretation The primary population result is negative; the subgroup should not be promoted into an overall treatment recommendation. The abstract reports no significant OS gain. Full subgroup interaction methods and contemporary PSMA staging applicability need review.

Primary article record: Williams S et al. J Clin Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1200/JCO-26-01794 | PMID 42921729

LITESPARK-011 | PFS benefit, final OS boundary missed

Practice informing

After anti-PD-(L)1 therapy; 747 assigned (371/376). Belzutifan/lenvatinib versus cabozantinib: PFS HR 0.74 (95% CI 0.62–0.88); ORR 53.1% versus 40.2%; response duration 22.0 versus 11.9 months. OS 33.7 versus 28.6 months; HR 0.85 (0.70–1.03), one-sided P=0.04689 against a prespecified boundary of 0.0223. Grade ≥3 TEAEs: 85.4% versus 83.0%.

Clinical interpretation Report durable PFS/response benefit and non-significant final OS separately. P<0.05 is insufficient when the prespecified boundary is stricter. Anaemia/hypoxia and lenvatinib toxicity need detailed supplement extraction before benefit–risk sign-off.

Primary article record: Heng DYC et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.109 | PMID 42927855

AZUR-1 | durable complete responses in dMMR/MSI-H rectal cancer

Practice informing

Single-arm registrational study; 154 enrolled, 144 with ≥12 months follow-up; median follow-up 26.3 months. Investigator-assessed sustained cCR for 12 months: 68%. Composite sustained cCR plus pCR: 76%; 12/15 patients undergoing surgery after dostarlimab alone had pCR. Of patients achieving cCR, 98% maintained it for ≥12 months. No recurrences were reported among cCR/pCR patients. Grade ≥3 immune-mediated AEs: four patients (3%). Central assessment was less mature.

Clinical interpretation The durable cCR endpoint and the composite including surgical pCR are different outcomes. This is not evidence that every patient avoids surgery. Delivery requires expert MRI, endoscopy, pathology and salvage planning; MMR/MSI confirmation is essential.

Negative

Practice informing

AMPLIFY-7P | primary DFS endpoint missed

What did NOT work

• Perioperative checkpoint-inhibitor intensification was negative in KEYNOTE-866 and CA017-078.

• ENZARAD did not establish overall MFS benefit; LITESPARK-012 did not support first-line triplet intensification in RCC.

• IROCAS did not improve adjuvant DFS; AMPLIFY-7P missed its primary DFS endpoint.

• Final OS did not meet the prespecified boundary in LITESPARK-011; postMONARCH and KEYNOTE-B49 did not establish OS improvement.

• MOIO did not establish non-inferiority of 12-week ICI dosing, with an investigator-reported survival concern. Original results verification remains pending.

Regulatory and Greek implementation

Regulatory verification for the added regimens is open. Trial results, manuscript acceptance and sponsor announcements do not establish an EMA/European Commission indication or Greek reimbursement. Before a local prescribing recommendation, confirm the product information and applicable EOF/EOPYY access. No new indication-level regulatory decision is certified by this revision.

Priorities for Greek centres include validated MMR/MSI, MET and PTEN testing; multidisciplinary pathways for perioperative treatments; CRS/neurotoxicity monitoring for T-cell engagers; and marrow, skin, neuropathy, metabolic and pulmonary toxicity support for ADCs. Assay associations alone do not validate a treatment-selection strategy.

What to watch next

• Original abstracts, protocols and safety supplements for the newly included congress items; OncologyPRO lists congress-resource availability from 30 October.

• Published primary manuscripts and the authoritative full simultaneous-publication list; acceptance must remain distinct from publication.

• Randomised ovarian ADC results and mature DOR/PFS; final OS and prespecified boundaries in endometrial studies.

• Independent confirmation of local regulatory status, biomarker eligibility and practical toxicity-management requirements.

3 take-home messages

• Match each result to its exact population, endpoint, data cut and prespecified statistical test.

• Read relative efficacy with absolute outcomes, severe toxicity, treatment exposure and applicability to current comparators.

• Emerging congress data can inform discussion while original-source checks and local access verification remain explicit.
