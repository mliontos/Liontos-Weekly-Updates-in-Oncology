Early

ZW191 (LBA40). Eighty patients received 6.4 mg/kg (n=37) or 9.6 mg/kg (n=43), with a median four prior lines and no FRα selection. Excluding clear-cell histology, response-evaluable populations were 31 and 38: ORR was 58% at each dose, but confirmed ORR was 52% and 45% (48% pooled). Including clear-cell cases, confirmed ORR was 47% and 41% (44% pooled; n=78). Median DOR was not reached; PFS was not provided. Grade ≥3 neutropenia was 8% versus 23%, anaemia 8% versus 14%, and pleural effusion 5% versus 9%.

JSKN033 (1243RO). Among 64 enrolled patients with pretreated cervical cancer, efficacy denominators were 34 at 8.4 mg and 28 at 6.7 mg, across schedules. ORR was 18/34 (52.9%) versus 5/28 (17.9%); at 8.4 mg, DCR was 88.2% and median PFS 7.2 months. The abstract does not explain why efficacy denominators total 62. Prior immunotherapy, bevacizumab and ADC exposure occurred in 75%, 56.3% and 10.9%; HER2 IHC was 0 in 71.9%. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 26.6%; injection reactions in 95.3% (grade ≥3, 1.6%); ILD in one patient. No treatment-related dose reduction, discontinuation or death was reported.

Clinical interpretation Small, noncomparative dose cohorts do not support a cross-ADC efficacy ranking. Confirmed response, histology and efficacy denominators must remain explicit.
