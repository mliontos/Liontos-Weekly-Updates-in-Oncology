COMPANION-002 | response/PFS benefit with substantial toxicity

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGastrointestinal cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Randomised phase 2/3; 168 patients (111/57), tovecimig/paclitaxel versus paclitaxel. ORR: 18.0% versus 5.3%, P=0.0228; PFS: 4.7 versus 2.6 months, HR 0.44, P<0.0001. OS HR 1.05, P=0.779. Crossover occurred in 31 control patients (54%). Grade ≥3 TEAEs: 93.5% versus 64.2%; hypertension occurred in 69.4% with the combination.

Clinical interpretation PFS and response improved; no OS gain was demonstrated. Crossover affects interpretation but does not prove a hidden OS benefit. Complete HR CIs and treatment-related fatal-event extraction remain open.

Primary article record: Azad NS et al. J Clin Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1200/JCO-26-02188 | PMID 42920414
