PANKU-Lung01 | iza-bren after third-generation EGFR TKI

Phase 3 in China; 432 randomised, 422 treated in the modified ITT analysis. BICR PFS: 11.1 months (95% CI 9.2–12.7) versus 5.7 (5.5–5.9); HR 0.40 (0.31–0.51), one-sided P<0.0001. Grade ≥3 TRAEs: 79% (170/216) versus 62% (128/206); treatment-related deaths: one each. Drug-related ILD: two patients (1%) versus none.

Clinical interpretation Promising bispecific ADC activity with substantial haematological toxicity. Preserve the modified-ITT definition and distinguish randomised from treated denominators. Do not compare its HR directly with SAFFRON, which selected MET-mediated resistance.

Primary article record: Hong S et al. Lancet (2026). DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(26)02026-X | PMID 42933461
