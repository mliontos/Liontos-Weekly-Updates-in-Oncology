PROTEUS substudy | perioperative apalutamide versus surgery alone

23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewProstate cancer
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Patients were randomised 1:3 to apalutamide 240 mg daily plus ADT for six cycles before and after radical prostatectomy/PLND (n=102), or immediate surgery (n=306). At 37.8 months’ median follow-up, median EFS was not reached versus 19.1 months; HR 0.38 (95% CI 0.26–0.55; P<0.0001). EFS included biochemical failure, local/regional recurrence, distant metastasis or death.

MFS by conventional or PSMA-PET imaging favoured intensification: HR 0.49 (0.26–0.90). Time to subsequent therapy HR: 0.29 (0.20–0.43). The presentation specifies nominal P values for secondary/exploratory endpoints.

Clinical interpretation The surgery-alone comparator addresses a different question from the main PROTEUS trial, which used placebo plus ADT. EFS success is the central finding; MFS is secondary, and an OS benefit is not established. Greater systemic toxicity and multidisciplinary patient selection must accompany the efficacy interpretation.
