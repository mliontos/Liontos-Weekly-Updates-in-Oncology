Confirmatory source verification audit

29 October 2026

← Weekly overview

Direct original-abstract checks completed for 50/58 review entries, covering 51 abstracts (one entry combines ZW191 and JSKN033). Eight entries remain without a matched same-analysis original abstract in this pass. This is not a completed 48-publication census audit or a verification of every full journal article and supplement.

Method: original congress session pages were opened, the abstracts expanded, and their reported populations, cutoffs, endpoint estimates and safety definitions compared with the existing review. Primary journal records already cited in each review were retained; this pass did not obtain every full text, protocol or supplement. Congress access did not require a sign-in. No claim of using a previously authenticated session is made.

Material qualifications: DUO-E interim OS remains non-significant under its prespecified testing despite nominal P=0.037 for the olaparib arm; MOIO contains an unresolved futility-rule inconsistency; JNJ-6143 has distinct dated analyses; ENERGIZE and ARTEMIS have unresolved safety-rate/causality differences between source records. HEALER, FourLight-1 and INTerpath use different safety attribution definitions across reports. All numerical findings are described with their source and analysis population in the linked reviews.
