BEHOLD-1 | mocertatug rezetecan in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer

Full Evidence Review
Early

Mo-Rez is a B7-H4-directed ADC carrying a TOP1 inhibitor. In the open-label phase 1b dose-selection component, 136 patients were randomised to 2.8, 4.8 or 5.8 mg/kg every 3 weeks; median prior lines were two (range 1–5). At the 30 June 2026 cutoff, median follow-up was 13.4 months in each group. The primary endpoint was investigator-assessed confirmed ORR per RECIST 1.1.

Among 135 treated patients, grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 64 (47%) overall and 32 (71%) at 5.8 mg/kg. TRAE discontinuations were 3% overall and 2% at 5.8 mg/kg. Randomised efficacy denominators and the treated safety denominator differ. ILD and fatal-event rates are not specified in this supplied abstract.

Clinical interpretation The selected 5.8-mg/kg dose has encouraging response durability and PFS, alongside substantial severe treatment-related toxicity. Randomisation among doses does not provide a standard-treatment comparator or establish cross-ADC superiority. Expansion at 5.8 mg/kg was ongoing at the time of reporting. Biomarker findings below use an earlier cutoff and should not be pooled with this clinical update.
