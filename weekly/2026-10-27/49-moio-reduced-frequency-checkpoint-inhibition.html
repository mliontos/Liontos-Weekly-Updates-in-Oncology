MOIO | reduced-frequency checkpoint inhibition

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewCross-tumour, melanoma and rare cancers
Full Evidence Review
Pending verification

The investigating institution reports 340 participants in a phase 3 study of standard versus 12-weekly ICI administration after response to six months of treatment. The interim analysis did not demonstrate non-inferiority. An OS signal led the independent committee to recommend early stopping; formal inferiority was not established.

Clinical interpretation These findings do not support uniform 12-week dosing in this population. Exact PFS/OS estimates, analysis populations, non-inferiority boundaries and severe/fatal safety require original LBA10/SAP verification. Conflicting rounded figures and P-value sidedness in secondary reports are not used here.

Source status: Institut Paoli-Calmettes investigator report, 27 October 2026; Gravis G et al., ESMO 2026 LBA10. Source / locator
