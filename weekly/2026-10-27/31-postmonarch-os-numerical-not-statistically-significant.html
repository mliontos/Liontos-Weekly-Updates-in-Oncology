postMONARCH | OS numerical, not statistically significant

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewBreast cancer
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Phase 3; 368 assigned (182/186), approximately 37 months follow-up. OS: 32.1 versus 24.6 months; HR 0.80 (95% CI 0.60–1.06), P=0.11. Updated PFS HR 0.78 (0.62–0.99), nominal P=0.039. Safety was described as consistent with the established abemaciclib profile, without updated numerical rates in the abstract.

Clinical interpretation The 7.5-month difference between OS medians should not be presented as a proven survival benefit. Maintain the distinction between the original PFS evidence and this non-significant OS update.

Primary article record: Kalinsky K et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.1283 | PMID 42926108
