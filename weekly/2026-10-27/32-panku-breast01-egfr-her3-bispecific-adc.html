PANKU-Breast01 | EGFR/HER3 bispecific ADC

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewBreast cancer
Full Evidence Review

Phase 3 in China; 383 treated after endocrine therapy, CDK4/6 inhibition and one or two chemotherapy lines. Iza-bren PFS: 8.2 months (95% CI 6.9–8.6) versus 4.0 (2.9–4.3); HR 0.41 (0.33–0.53), P<0.0001. Grade ≥3 TRAEs: 85% versus 66%; 73% received platelet-elevating agents and 15% platelet transfusions with iza-bren. Two treatment-related deaths occurred in the experimental arm. OS was immature.

Clinical interpretation Substantial PFS activity accompanies considerable marrow toxicity and resource use. Global generalisability, sequencing against available ADCs and the evolving comparator landscape need assessment. A favourable PFS HR does not establish OS benefit.

Primary article record: Lan B et al. Lancet (2026). DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(26)02096-9 | PMID 42933459
