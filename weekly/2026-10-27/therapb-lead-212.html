TheraPb | lead-212 PSMA-targeted radiopharmaceutical

23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewProstate cancer
Full Evidence Review
Early

This phase 1b dose/schedule study enrolled 22 pretreated PSMA-positive patients; median follow-up was 14.1 months. No DLTs occurred and the maximum tolerated dose was not reached. At selected doses of 160/200 MBq, PSA50 occurred in 12/15 (80%); all five RECIST-evaluable patients responded. Median rPFS was 11.2 months (95% CI 2.0–not estimable).

Treatment-related dry mouth affected 18/22 (82%) and nausea 14/22 (64%), all grade 1–2. No grade 4–5 or serious TRAEs were reported; the abstract does not state that grade 3 TRAEs were absent.

Clinical interpretation Five evaluable radiographic responders do not imply a 100% response rate for all treated patients. Dose/schedule selection and wide uncertainty preclude comparisons with established lutetium-PSMA regimens.
