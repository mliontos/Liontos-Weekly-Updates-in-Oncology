Gedatolisib plus darolutamide | early mCRPC data

23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewProstate cancer
Full Evidence Review
Early

At the 14 August 2026 cutoff, the phase 1b/2 programme had treated 45 patients across gedatolisib 120/180/240-mg cohorts (19/19/7), combined with darolutamide 600 mg twice daily. Prior-abiraterone patients (n=22) had median rPFS 15.7 months and six-month rPFS 82.2%; prior-enzalutamide patients (n=18) had corresponding results of 9.1 months and 62.5%.

Grade 3 TRAEs occurred in 9/45 (20%), including one dose-limiting grade 3 rash. No grade 4/5 TRAEs or TRAEs leading to discontinuation/death were reported.

Clinical interpretation Dose randomisation is not randomisation against an active standard treatment. The prior-treatment subsets are small, do not cover all 45 patients, and must not be compared causally. Confidence intervals were not supplied in the release. These figures are from the later sponsor update; the earlier original abstract is reconciled below.

Sources and verification status

Celcuity. Results release, 23 October 2026; ESMO 2026 poster 2237P; CELC-G-201.

Sponsor update and original ESMO abstract independently checked; a full journal results report has not been verified.

Original ESMO 2237P checked, 29 October 2026. The abstract prints its earlier cutoff as “05/03/2026” and reports median follow-up 7.7 months. The same 45-patient dose cohorts had grade 3 TRAEs in 7/45 (16%), one dose-limiting grade 3 rash, and no grade 4 TRAEs, treatment-related discontinuations or deaths. Confirmed PSA50 was 26.7% (95% CI 14.6–41.9) overall and 40.9% (20.7–63.6) after abiraterone. Median PFS was 9.1 months (5.8–10.9) overall and 15.7 (7.7–NR) after abiraterone. The later 14 August sponsor update above has different safety/response estimates and prior-treatment subsets; these analyses are not interchangeable. Original abstract; NCT06190899.
