CLARITY-Gastric01 | sonesitatug vedotin in 2L+ CLDN18.2-positive gastroesophageal carcinoma

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGastrointestinal cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

In this global, randomised, open-label phase 3 study, eligibility required ≥1 prior therapy and CLDN18.2 membranous staining at any intensity in ≥25% of tumour cells. The trial initially randomised patients 1:1:1 to Sone-Ve 1.8 mg/kg, Sone-Ve 2.2 mg/kg or investigator’s choice; after dose selection, randomisation was 1:1 to Sone-Ve 2.2 mg/kg or investigator’s choice. At the 31 May 2026 cutoff, 532 of 594 total patients comprised the selected-dose comparison (267 versus 265); median follow-up was 8.6 months.

Dual primary endpoints were OS in 3L+ and BICR PFS in the 2L+ ITT population; OS in ITT was a key secondary endpoint. Investigator’s choice was ramucirumab plus paclitaxel, paclitaxel or docetaxel in 2L, and irinotecan, trifluridine/tipiracil or apatinib (China only) in 3L+.

Clinical interpretation Sone-Ve significantly improved OS in the 3L+ primary population and in the overall 2L+ ITT population, while the BICR PFS co-primary endpoint was not met. Grade ≥3 TRAEs were less frequent overall, with more severe gastrointestinal toxicity and less severe haematological toxicity than investigator’s choice. Fatal TRAEs occurred in both arms. The results support further consideration as a 2L+ option across the trial’s CLDN18.2 eligibility range; local regulatory/access status and journal-level verification remain open.
