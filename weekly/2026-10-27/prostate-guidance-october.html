Prostate guidance | ASTRO hypofractionation and APCCC 2026

23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewProstate cancer
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

The ASTRO guideline recommends moderate hypofractionation over conventional fractionation across risk groups. Ultrahypofractionation is a standard option in low/intermediate-risk disease and may be considered in selected high-risk patients, preferably in trials; baseline urinary dysfunction may favour moderate hypofractionation. Contemporary image-guided conformal techniques remain central.

The APCCC report reached consensus on 44/134 questions (32.8%) and strong consensus on seven, using thresholds of ≥75% and ≥90% agreement. Its 133 voting panellists addressed areas of uncertainty; the report is not a formal systematic review or meta-analysis.

Clinical interpretation Guideline recommendations and expert votes answer different questions from trial results. APCCC voting should not be treated as new comparative efficacy evidence or assumed to incorporate all newly reported ESMO results.
