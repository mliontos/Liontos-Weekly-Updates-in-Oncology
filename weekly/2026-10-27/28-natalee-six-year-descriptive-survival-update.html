NATALEE | six-year descriptive survival update

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewBreast cancer
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Adjuvant ribociclib/NSAI versus NSAI. Median OS follow-up 66.5 months. Six-year absolute OS difference 2.1 points; HR 0.809 (95% CI 0.661–0.989), nominal one-sided P=0.019. Six-year iDFS difference 5.3 points; HR 0.741 (0.646–0.849), nominal one-sided P<0.0001. DDFS HR 0.744 (0.646–0.858); DRFS HR 0.738 (0.634–0.858). No new safety signal was reported, without updated numerical toxicity rates in the abstract.

Clinical interpretation Encouraging long-term outcome consistency, but this OS analysis is explicitly descriptive. Preserve the nominal, one-sided character of the P values. Do not describe it as a newly alpha-controlled OS success.

Primary article record: Slamon DJ et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.1288 | PMID 42918423
