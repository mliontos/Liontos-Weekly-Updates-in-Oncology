TroFuse-005 | sacituzumab tirumotecan after platinum and immunotherapy

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGynaecological cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Randomised phase 3; 776 assigned (386/390), with safety populations of 384/367. Median follow-up 14.0 months. PFS: 7.2 months (95% CI 5.6–7.6) versus 3.9 (3.7–5.3); HR 0.59 (0.50–0.70), P<0.0001. OS: 19.0 months (16.1–not reached) versus 12.1 (11.0–15.2); HR 0.68 (0.55–0.84), P=0.0001. Grade ≥3 all-cause AEs: 69% versus 57%; serious AEs: 35% versus 32%; treatment-related deaths: three in each arm. Randomisation incorporated MMR and TROP2 expression.

Clinical interpretation Strong evidence of efficacy in the post-platinum/post-IO setting, with greater severe toxicity. Median differences are 3.3 months for PFS and 6.9 for OS; these are descriptive differences between medians. Do not require a TROP2 threshold without checking the final label. EU authorisation and Greek reimbursement for this indication remain unverified.

Primary article record: Lorusso D et al. Lancet (2026). DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(26)02099-4 | PMID 42933460
