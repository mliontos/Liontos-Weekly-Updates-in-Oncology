The 22 October sponsor release for the congress presentation reports 1,137 patients and median follow-up 24.6 months. Intismeran/pembrolizumab versus pembrolizumab: RFS HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.45–0.75), two-sided P<0.001; DMFS HR 0.62 (0.45–0.85), P=0.002. Two-year RFS: 82.2% versus 70.0%; DMFS: 88.5% versus 80.2%. Grade 3–5 TRAEs: 14.2% versus 13.4%. OS was immature.

Clinical interpretation Promising phase 3 evidence for recurrence reduction. The source describes an accepted NEJM manuscript; original LBA1 is now directly verified, while the separately published article/DOI remains unresolved in this review. Retain immature OS and manufacturing/logistical questions. This is a congress-linked update despite the pre-window release date.

Source status: Merck/Moderna public results release, 22 October 2026; ESMO 2026, LBA1. Source / locator
