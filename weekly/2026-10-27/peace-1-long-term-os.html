PEACE-1 | long-term overall survival with abiraterone

23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewProstate cancer
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

The 2×2 factorial phase 3 trial enrolled 1,173 patients, testing abiraterone/prednisone and/or prostate radiotherapy with ADT-based standard care. At 7.7 years’ median follow-up, the overall abiraterone OS HR was 0.75 (95% CI 0.65–0.87; P=0.0001).

Subgroup OS HR: 0.68 (0.56–0.82). The volume interaction P value was 0.56; low-volume HR 0.74 (0.52–1.05) remained imprecise. Updated safety rates were not provided in the abstract.

Clinical interpretation This is durable follow-up of an established intensification strategy. It does not compare chemotherapy triplets against contemporary ARPI doublets. Lack of an interaction is not proof of identical benefit by volume; the low-volume estimate should retain its uncertainty.
