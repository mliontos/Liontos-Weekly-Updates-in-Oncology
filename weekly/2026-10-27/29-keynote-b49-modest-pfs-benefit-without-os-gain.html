KEYNOTE-B49 | modest PFS benefit without OS gain

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewBreast cancer
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

PD-L1 CPS≥1 HR+/HER2-negative advanced disease; 342 patients. PFS: 6.7 versus 6.0 months; HR 0.74 (95% CI 0.58–0.95), P=0.0089, meeting the trial criterion. Final OS: 21.0 versus 22.2 months; HR 1.04 (0.81–1.33), P=0.6185. CPS≥10 PFS HR 0.59 (0.35–0.98). Grade ≥3 TRAEs: 38% versus 33%; TRAE discontinuations: 11% versus 5%.

Clinical interpretation PD-L1 selection did not establish an OS benefit in this setting. The median PFS difference is 0.7 months; discuss that absolute scale alongside the HR. The smaller CPS≥10 subgroup is not a separate confirmatory result.

Primary article record: Rugo HS et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.1285 | PMID 42926111
