AZUR-1 | durable complete responses in dMMR/MSI-H rectal cancer

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGastrointestinal cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Single-arm registrational study; 154 enrolled, 144 with ≥12 months follow-up; median follow-up 26.3 months. Investigator-assessed sustained cCR for 12 months: 68%. Composite sustained cCR plus pCR: 76%; 12/15 patients undergoing surgery after dostarlimab alone had pCR. Of patients achieving cCR, 98% maintained it for ≥12 months. No recurrences were reported among cCR/pCR patients. Grade ≥3 immune-mediated AEs: four patients (3%). Central assessment was less mature.

Clinical interpretation The durable cCR endpoint and the composite including surgical pCR are different outcomes. This is not evidence that every patient avoids surgery. Delivery requires expert MRI, endoscopy, pathology and salvage planning; MMR/MSI confirmation is essential.

Primary article record: Cercek A et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.1280 | PMID 42927858
