JNJ-6143 | phase 1 RIPTAC activity in mCRPC

23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewProstate cancer
Full Evidence Review
Early

JNJ-6143 links AR and BRD4 through the RIPTAC mechanism. At 21 September 2026, 105 patients had been treated across doses, including 38 at 50 mg. In that cohort, confirmed PSA50 was 24/31 (77%) and PSA90 15/31 (48%); confirmed ORR was 11/16 (69%), with median DOR 11.0 months (95% CI 5.9–NE). The slide notes one post-cutoff SD-to-PR conversion; the subsequent sponsor release reports 12/16 (75%).

At cutoff, grade ≥3 TRAEs were 2/38 (5%) at 50 mg and 18/105 (17%) overall. A footnote reclassified both severe 50-mg events as unrelated after cutoff. Across all doses, one treatment-related death from acute kidney injury was reported; none occurred at 50 mg. TRAE discontinuations: 1/38 (3%) and 5/105 (5%).

Clinical interpretation This is early dose-selected evidence. PSA and radiographic response denominators differ from the safety population. Maintain the original versus post-cutoff distinction for response and causality; neither response rate establishes survival benefit.
