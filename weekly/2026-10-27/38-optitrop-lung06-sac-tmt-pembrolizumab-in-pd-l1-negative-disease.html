OptiTROP-Lung06 | sac-TMT/pembrolizumab in PD-L1-negative disease

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026

Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Phase 3, nonsquamous NSCLC without targetable alterations; 455 assigned (229/226), follow-up 9.7 months. PFS: 15.0 versus 5.7 months; HR 0.49 (95% CI 0.36–0.65), P<0.001. ORR: 50.2% versus 47.3%; DOR: 13.6 versus 6.8 months. Immature OS HR 0.58 (0.38–0.88). Grade ≥3 post-treatment AEs: 47.6% versus 60.6%; stomatitis was common, mostly grade 1–2.

Clinical interpretation The PFS improvement is substantial, whereas ORR differs little and OS remains immature. A nominal OS CI excluding 1 does not establish a multiplicity-controlled OS success. Population and PD-L1 threshold must remain specific to this trial.

Primary article record: Zhou F et al. N Engl J Med (2026). DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2611280 | PMID 42929694
