KEYNOTE-866 | negative EFS result

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGenitourinary cancers
Full Evidence Review
Negative

Phase 3; 907 assigned. At 60.9 months median follow-up, 60-month EFS was 63.5% versus 61.9%; HR 0.88 (95% CI 0.71–1.10), one-sided P=0.1366. pCR: 44.4% versus 35.8%; model-estimated difference 8.9 points (2.5–15.1). OS HR 1.00 (0.79–1.27). Grade ≥3 TEAEs: 80.2% versus 75.1%.

Clinical interpretation Perioperative pembrolizumab added to neoadjuvant cisplatin/gemcitabine did not improve the primary EFS endpoint. A higher pCR rate does not override the negative survival result. Do not infer a uniform class effect across perioperative checkpoint inhibitors.
