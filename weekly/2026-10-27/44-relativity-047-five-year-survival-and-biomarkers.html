RELATIVITY-047 | five-year survival and biomarkers

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewCross-tumour, melanoma and rare cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Phase 2/3; nivolumab/relatlimab n=355 versus nivolumab n=359. Minimum follow-up 57.3 months. PFS HR 0.77 (95% CI 0.65–0.92); OS HR 0.78 (0.64–0.95); melanoma-specific survival HR 0.71 (0.57–0.88). The abstract reports consistent safety without updated rates. High LAG3-positive CD8-cell levels were associated with poorer nivolumab outcomes in exploratory biomarker-evaluable PD-L1≥1% analyses.

Clinical interpretation Long-term follow-up supports durable benefit. Melanoma-specific survival is post hoc and the biomarker findings are exploratory. LAG3/CD8 findings do not constitute a validated selection test; retrieve absolute survival and detailed toxicity from the full article.

Primary article record: Long GV et al. Nat Med (2026). DOI 10.1038/s41591-026-04756-1 | PMID 42926136
