LITESPARK-012 | first-line triplet intensification unsuccessful

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGenitourinary cancers
Full Evidence Review
Negative

1,688 assigned: pembrolizumab/lenvatinib plus belzutifan (A, n=560), plus quavonlimab (B, n=562), or doublet control (n=566). Median PFS: 20.8, 20.7 and 23.7 months; HRs versus control 1.08 (95% CI 0.93–1.26; P=0.8408) and 1.07 (0.92–1.25; nominal P=0.8174). OS HRs: 1.04 (0.87–1.25; P=0.6622) and 1.12 (0.94–1.35; nominal P=0.8955). AEs causing any-drug discontinuation: 51.6%, 51.4%, 36.2%.

Clinical interpretation Neither intensification improved outcomes; discontinuation was more frequent. Preserve these negative results alongside favourable post-IO findings from LITESPARK-011: they concern different populations and treatment questions.

Primary article record: Rini BI et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.1281 | PMID 42927856
