Sofetabart mipitecan plus bevacizumab | platinum-sensitive ovarian cancer

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGynaecological cancers
Full Evidence Review
Early

In this phase 1 cohort, 33 patients received the FRα-targeted, exatecan-payload ADC Sofe-M 4 mg/kg plus bevacizumab 15 mg/kg. Eligibility required ECOG 0–1, with no limit on prior therapies and prior mirvetuximab allowed. At the 5 March 2026 cutoff, median age was 67 years (41–83), median prior therapies three (1–6), 70% had prior bevacizumab and 67% prior PARPi.

At median follow-up of six months, reported ORR was 64% (95% CI 45–80): three CRs, 16 PRs and two unconfirmed PRs. This reported ORR therefore must not be labelled a confirmed ORR. DCR was 100% (95% CI 89–100); 18/21 responders (86%) remained on treatment and median DOR was not reached. Numerical PFS was not supplied.

Common TEAEs were nausea 73%, epistaxis 67%, fatigue 64%, anaemia 58% and neutropenia 55%. Grade ≥3 neutropenia occurred in 39.4%, including grade 3 febrile neutropenia in 3%. Two patients (6.1%) had grade 2 ILD. One patient (3%) discontinued both agents due to a TEAE. No keratopathies or high-grade peripheral neuropathy were reported; a grade ≥3 anaemia rate and overall severe/fatal TEAE rates were not supplied.

Clinical interpretation The activity signal is promising in a heavily pretreated PSOC cohort, including after PARPi, but response confirmation, immature durability, ILD and marrow toxicity matter. Tiny FRα subgroups and non-randomised prior-PARPi comparisons cannot establish equivalence. This entry uses the 5 March abstract cutoff and replaces the previous later sponsor-update figures; the two cuts must not be blended. The abstract states that phase 3 FRAmework-01 (NCT07213804) was enrolling at reporting.
