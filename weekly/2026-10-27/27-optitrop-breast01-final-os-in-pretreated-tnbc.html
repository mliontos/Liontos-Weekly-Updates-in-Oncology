OptiTROP-Breast01 | final OS in pretreated TNBC

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026

Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Phase 3; 263 randomised (130/133), follow-up 29.4 months. OS: 14.7 months (95% CI 12.1–20.1) versus 9.9 (8.6–11.8); HR 0.60 (0.45–0.80). Thirty-month OS: 26.8% versus 13.2%. Subsequent ADC use occurred in 27.8% of controls; crossover-adjusted model HR 0.55 (0.39–0.73). Main grade ≥3 TRAEs were neutropenia, leukopenia and anaemia; updated rates were absent from the abstract.

Clinical interpretation Primary article-record verification resolves the earlier unverified OS figures. Lead with the unadjusted randomised estimate; crossover models are sensitivity analyses. Do not import a P value or numerical toxicity rate absent from the retrieved primary record.
