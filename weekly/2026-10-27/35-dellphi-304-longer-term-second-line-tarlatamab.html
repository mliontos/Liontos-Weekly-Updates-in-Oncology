DeLLphi-304 | longer-term second-line tarlatamab

Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Updated phase 3 after platinum. OS: 13.6 versus 8.4 months; PFS: 4.2 versus 3.2 months. Twenty-four-month OS: 30.3% versus 14.9%; 24-month PFS: 14.2% versus 2.3%. ORR: 35.4% versus 20.4%. TRAE discontinuations: 3.2% versus 6.6%; CRS and ICANS predominantly occurred early and were low grade. Updated HRs/CIs were not supplied in the retrieved abstract.

Clinical interpretation Durable outcome separation supports the existing second-line evidence. This is not the DeLLphi-305 maintenance population. Do not transfer HRs or survival clocks between the trials.

Primary article record: Rudin CM et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.095 | PMID 42933636
