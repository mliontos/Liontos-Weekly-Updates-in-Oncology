CAPItello-281 | updated descriptive OS

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGenitourinary cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

PTEN-deficient de novo mHSPC; 1,012 patients. At an ad hoc cut-off with 42% OS maturity, OS was 57.4 versus 46.5 months; HR 0.82 (95% CI 0.68–0.99), P=0.044. Updated rPFS: 35.5 versus 26.0 months; HR 0.83 (0.70–0.99), nominal P=0.033. The original rPFS analysis had HR 0.81 (0.66–0.98), P=0.034. PTEN deficiency required absent specific cytoplasmic staining in ≥90% of viable malignant cells.

Clinical interpretation The OS update is ad hoc and descriptive, despite a CI excluding 1. ESMO LBA56 explicitly states that this alpha-controlled OS analysis did not meet statistical significance; the updated rPFS analysis was not formally tested. Do not present it as a new confirmatory OS success. Updated numerical safety rates require the full article; “no new signals” does not replace those rates. Confirm assay applicability before local adoption.

Primary article record: Fizazi K et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.097 | PMID 42918424
