BEHOLD-1 biomarkers | B7-H4 expression and the tumour microenvironment

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGynaecological cancers
Full Evidence Review
Early

This exploratory analysis used screening archival/fresh tissue from BEHOLD-1: B7-H4 IHC in 111 tumours and whole-transcriptome sequencing in 79. Response associations were assessed across all doses at the earlier 12 November 2025 cutoff. Responders had a confirmed response; non-responders included stable/progressive disease and unevaluable patients. The supplied abstract describes this as phase 1b dose expansion, whereas LBA41 describes dose selection; cohort overlap is not resolved.

B7-H4 was expressed in 96% of tumours but was not associated with response. Immune-related pathways were enriched in responders; TNFα–NF-κB signalling had the highest reported enrichment (NES 2.6). Responders had higher macrophage (P=0.02), Treg/Th2 traffic (P=0.0065), myeloid-cell traffic (P=0.049) and matrix-remodelling (P=0.00065) signature scores. Cathepsin B (CTSB) expression was higher in responders (P=0.02). CTSB, macrophage and matrix-remodelling signatures correlated with each other (r=0.5–0.63; P<0.00001).

Clinical interpretation These associations support a hypothesis that a macrophage-rich, matrix-remodelling microenvironment and CTSB facilitate linker cleavage, payload release and bystander activity. This mechanism is a proposed interpretation, not demonstrated causality. High target prevalence did not translate into a response-selection biomarker in this analysis. No validated composite assay, clinical cutoff, independent validation or multiplicity adjustment is supplied; these exploratory signatures should not determine treatment eligibility.

Lheureux S, Salutari V, Oaknin A, et al. BEHOLD-1: exploratory analysis of biomarkers associated with mocertatug rezetecan response in patients with PROC. ESMO Congress 2026; abstract 1248RO.
