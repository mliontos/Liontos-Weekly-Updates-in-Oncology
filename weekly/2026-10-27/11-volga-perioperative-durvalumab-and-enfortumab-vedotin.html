VOLGA | perioperative durvalumab and enfortumab vedotin

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGenitourinary cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Phase 3 in cisplatin-ineligible/refusing MIBC; durvalumab–EV n=233, durvalumab–tremelimumab–EV n=233, control n=229. Doublet versus control: 24-month EFS 63.5% (95% CI 56.5–69.7) versus 45.1% (38.2–51.8); HR 0.51 (0.38–0.67), P<0.001. OS at 24 months: 78.4% versus 69.4%; HR 0.58 (0.40–0.83), P=0.003. Triplet EFS at 24 months: 60.1%; HR versus control 0.61 (0.47–0.80), P<0.001. Grade ≥3 AEs: 71.1%, 79.5%, 50.2%; grade ≥3 TRAEs in the experimental arms: 30.7%, 44.5%.

Clinical interpretation The doublet produced absolute 24-month EFS and OS gains of 18.4 and 9.0 percentage points. The trial does not establish an advantage for adding tremelimumab or superiority over another active perioperative EV/IO regimen. Assess skin, neuropathy, glucose and immune toxicity in implementation planning.

Primary article record: Powles T et al. N Engl J Med (2026). DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2608323 | PMID 42929692
