PRABITAS | simplified FTD-TPI/bevacizumab schedule

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGastrointestinal cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Japanese pragmatic non-inferiority phase 3; 970 analysed (484 on 14-day, 486 on 28-day schedule), follow-up 17.9 months. OS: 11.3 versus 10.3 months; HR 0.93 (95% CI 0.80–1.08); non-inferiority margin 1.25. Grade ≥3 neutropenia: 13% versus 39%; grade ≥2 anorexia: 15% versus 26%. Two treatment-related deaths occurred with the 14-day schedule versus none with the 28-day schedule.

Clinical interpretation Potentially useful optimisation that requires a faithful schedule description, local prescribing review and consideration of Japanese generalisability. It establishes non-inferiority within its margin, not OS superiority.

Primary article record: Sakakida T et al. Lancet (2026). DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(26)01782-4 | PMID 42925880
