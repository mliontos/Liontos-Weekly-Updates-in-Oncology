Randomised study; atirmociclib/fulvestrant n=126, investigator-choice treatment n=137. PFS: 9.4 versus 6.6 months; HR 0.60 (95% CI 0.440–0.825), one-sided P=0.0007. ORR among measurable disease: 19.3% versus 11.3%, nominal two-sided P=0.0901; DOR 14.8 versus 9.3 months. Any-grade neutropenia, leukopenia and diarrhoea with atirmociclib: 47.2%, 33.6%, 28.0%; TEAE discontinuation: 6.4%.

Clinical interpretation PFS benefit warrants sequencing attention. The ORR comparison is not significant; OS and complete grade ≥3 safety require further extraction. The investigator-choice regimens are verified below; their mixed control population matters for interpretation.

Primary article record: Giordano A et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.1282 | PMID 42926110

Source reconciliation. Original 1842O verifies the control regimens: fulvestrant alone (n=79) or everolimus/exemestane (n=58). The ORR comparison has one-sided P=0.0451 in the congress table versus nominal two-sided P=0.0901 in the article; this is not confirmatory two-sided ORR significance.
