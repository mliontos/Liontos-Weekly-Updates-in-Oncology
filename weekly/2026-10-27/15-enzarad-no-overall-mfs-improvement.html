ENZARAD | no overall MFS improvement

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGenitourinary cancers
Full Evidence Review
Negative

Phase 3; 802 patients receiving radiotherapy and ADT. At 92 months median follow-up, 8-year conventional-imaging MFS was 74% with enzalutamide versus 72% with an older antiandrogen; HR 0.88 (95% CI 0.67–1.15), P=0.34. Grade 3–5 AEs occurred in 185 patients in each arm. A planned pelvic-node radiotherapy subgroup had HR 0.47 (0.29–0.76), adjusted P=0.004.

Clinical interpretation The primary population result is negative; the subgroup should not be promoted into an overall treatment recommendation. The abstract reports no significant OS gain. Full subgroup interaction methods and contemporary PSMA staging applicability need review.

Primary article record: Williams S et al. J Clin Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1200/JCO-26-01794 | PMID 42921729
