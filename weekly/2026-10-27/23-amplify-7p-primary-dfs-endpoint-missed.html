Randomised phase 2 after standard therapy for KRAS-mutant stage I–III PDAC; camupepimut n=96 versus observation n=48. DFS: 23.8 versus 21.2 months; HR 0.85, P=0.5329. Prespecified ctDNA-negative subgroup HR 0.51, P=0.0280; no-prior-radiation subgroup HR 0.59, P=0.0221. A post hoc adjusted model gave HR 0.47, P=0.0086. Grade ≥3 AEs: 10.4% versus 14.6%; serious AEs: 6.3% versus 8.3%.

Clinical interpretation Lead with the negative primary result. Subgroup, adjusted-model and immune-response associations support future research, not a rescued positive trial. Several CIs and multiplicity details require full-text checking; do not fill these from secondary summaries.
