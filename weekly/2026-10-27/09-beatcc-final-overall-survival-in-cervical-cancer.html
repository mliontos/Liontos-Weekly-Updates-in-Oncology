BEATcc | final overall survival in cervical cancer

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGynaecological cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

BEATcc randomised 410 patients with metastatic, persistent or recurrent cervical cancer to chemotherapy and bevacizumab with or without atezolizumab. At final analysis, 277 deaths had occurred; median follow-up was 57.2 months (95% CI 54.9–59.5). Median OS was 31.9 versus 22.7 months; HR 0.69 (95% CI 0.54–0.88). Four-year OS was 40% versus 26%. Median PFS2 was 23.3 versus 17.0 months; HR 0.62 (0.49–0.78).

Exploratory OS HRs were 0.71 (0.50–0.99) for PD-L1 CPS ≥1 and 0.53 (0.32–0.88) for CPS <1. No interaction test is supplied. In the control arm, 64/110 patients receiving post-progression therapy received immunotherapy. No patients remained on study treatment; the abstract describes late safety as consistent with previous reports without numerical updated severe/fatal rates.

Clinical interpretation The final analysis supports a sustained survival benefit. Subgroup point estimates alone cannot establish equal benefit across PD-L1 strata. Indication-level EU/Greek access remains a separate implementation check.
