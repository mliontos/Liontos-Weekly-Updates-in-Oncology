START-001 | invikafusp alfa

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewCross-tumour, melanoma and rare cancers
Full Evidence Review
Early

Phase 1/2; 34 dose-escalation and 18 initial phase 2 participants. At the optimal biological dose cohorts, ORR was 4/27 (14.8%); no responses below 0.08 mg/kg. Two reversible dose-limiting toxicities occurred. CRS was the most common TRAE, predominantly grades 1–2. No treatment-related deaths or non-laboratory grade 4 AEs were reported. The recommended phase 2 dose was 0.08 mg/kg.

Clinical interpretation Early proof of mechanism for Vβ6/Vβ10-selective T-cell agonism. Dose-selected response proportions and heterogeneous tumour groups cannot establish tumour-specific efficacy. Complete severe-AE and CRS rates require further extraction.

Primary article record: Sullivan RJ et al. Nat Med (2026). DOI 10.1038/s41591-026-04711-0 | PMID 42919963
