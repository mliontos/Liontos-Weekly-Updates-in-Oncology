DeLLphi-305 | tarlatamab/durvalumab maintenance

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewThoracic cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

Phase 3; 563 assigned after no progression on initial chemoimmunotherapy. OS: 21.1 months (95% CI 20.8–not estimable) versus 12.7 (11.2–14.8); HR 0.42 (0.32–0.54), P<0.001. PFS: 7.4 months (5.6–9.1) versus 1.9 (1.9–2.0); HR 0.34 (0.28–0.42), P<0.001. Grade ≥3 AEs: 46.8% versus 40.6%. CRS occurred in approximately half of combination-treated patients, predominantly low grade.

Clinical interpretation Large efficacy benefit in a maintenance-selected population. Both medians begin at maintenance randomisation, not initial diagnosis or induction start. CRS/neurological monitoring capability, step-up administration and local access are central to implementation.

Primary article record: Pérol M et al. N Engl J Med (2026). DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2609482 | PMID 42929702
