Dalmelitinib/aumolertinib | dual EGFR/MET blockade

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026

Full Evidence Review
Watch

Randomised phase 3; 314 patients with EGFR-TKI resistance and MET amplification. PFS: 6.2 versus 4.4 months; HR 0.60 (95% CI 0.45–0.81), P<0.001. ORR: 59.6% versus 38.6%; DOR: 9.7 versus 4.4 months. Grade ≥3 TEAEs: 50.0% versus 51.0%, with more liver laboratory abnormalities on the targeted combination. OS was immature.

Clinical interpretation Primary publication adds to biomarker-selected dual inhibition. Verify prior TKI distribution, MET amplification assay and international applicability before combining it editorially with SAFFRON.

Primary article record: Li Z et al. J Clin Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1200/JCO-26-02432 | PMID 42928710
