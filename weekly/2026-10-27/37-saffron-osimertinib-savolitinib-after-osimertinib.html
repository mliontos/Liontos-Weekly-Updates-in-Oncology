SAFFRON | osimertinib/savolitinib after osimertinib

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewThoracic cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

EGFR-mutant NSCLC with MET overexpression/amplification; 338 randomised from 1,746 screened. PFS HR 0.55 (95% CI 0.42–0.72), P<0.001; final OS HR 0.68 (0.50–0.91), P=0.01. Twelve-month PFS: 36% versus 10%; 12-month OS: 70% versus 53%. Grade ≥3 treatment-emergent AEs: 64% versus 63%. Common experimental-arm AEs: peripheral oedema 49%, anaemia 26%, diarrhoea 26%.

Clinical interpretation Supports dual EGFR/MET inhibition in a selected resistance mechanism. MET overexpression and amplification should retain their trial-specific assay definitions; they are not interchangeable informal labels. Do not confuse all-cause AE rates with lower treatment-related rates quoted in some summaries.

Primary article record: Lu S et al. N Engl J Med (2026). DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2611335 | PMID 42929693
