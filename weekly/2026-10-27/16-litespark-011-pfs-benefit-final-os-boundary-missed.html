LITESPARK-011 | PFS benefit, final OS boundary missed

ESMO 2026
23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewGenitourinary cancers
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

After anti-PD-(L)1 therapy; 747 assigned (371/376). Belzutifan/lenvatinib versus cabozantinib: PFS HR 0.74 (95% CI 0.62–0.88); ORR 53.1% versus 40.2%; response duration 22.0 versus 11.9 months. OS 33.7 versus 28.6 months; HR 0.85 (0.70–1.03), one-sided P=0.04689 against a prespecified boundary of 0.0223. Grade ≥3 TEAEs: 85.4% versus 83.0%.

Clinical interpretation Report durable PFS/response benefit and non-significant final OS separately. P<0.05 is insufficient when the prespecified boundary is stricter. Anaemia/hypoxia and lenvatinib toxicity need detailed supplement extraction before benefit–risk sign-off.

Primary article record: Heng DYC et al. Ann Oncol (2026). DOI 10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.109 | PMID 42927855
