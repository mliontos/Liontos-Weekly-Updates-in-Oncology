DASL-HiCaP | darolutamide with ADT and radiotherapy

23–27 October 2026
← Weekly overviewProstate cancer
Full Evidence Review
Practice informing

The phase 3 trial randomised 1,106 men (553/arm) with very high-risk localised disease or biochemical persistence/recurrence within one year after prostatectomy. Darolutamide 600 mg twice daily or placebo was given for 96 weeks with 96 weeks of ADT and prostate/prostate-bed plus pelvic-node radiotherapy. Median follow-up: 3.8 years.

Clinical interpretation This is an important curative-intent MFS result, not a demonstrated OS benefit. Conventional imaging defines the endpoint; PSMA-PET staging can change applicability. ENZARAD is a different trial with different eligibility, comparator and treatment design; the two do not establish head-to-head ARPI superiority. The sponsor stated that this setting was not approved at the announcement; indication-level EU/Greek access still requires confirmation.
