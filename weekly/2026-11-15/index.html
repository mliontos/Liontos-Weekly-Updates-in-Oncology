Genitourinary cancers
FDA · 13 November · Regulatory milestone
Phase III Clinical Trials Regulatory Issues

VOLGA: perioperative EV + durvalumab in MIBC

Randomized phase III (695 patients) in cisplatin-ineligible or cisplatin-refusing MIBC. Neoadjuvant EV/durvalumab followed by radical cystectomy and adjuvant durvalumab versus cystectomy alone: median EFS not reached versus 12.1 months; HR 0.51 (95% CI 0.38–0.67; two-sided P<0.0001). OS HR 0.58 (0.40–0.83; P=0.0027), with median OS not reached in either arm.

Clinical interpretation: Demonstrated event-free and overall survival benefit versus cystectomy alone in the trial population. Does not establish comparative superiority to other active perioperative regimens. Toxicity monitoring must account for EV-associated skin, neuropathy and metabolic effects, plus immune-related AEs. US approval does not imply EU or Greek access.

TALAPRO-3: talazoparib + enzalutamide + ADT

CHMP recommended extension to adults with HRR gene-mutated mHSPC. The phase III trial reported a radiographic PFS advantage (previously reported HR 0.48, 95% CI 0.36–0.65). Evaluation by specific HRR alteration and hematologic toxicity remains critical.

Implementation: CHMP opinion only; European Commission decision, amended SmPC, testing access and national reimbursement are separate steps.

Phase III Clinical Trials Regulatory Issues

LITESPARK-011: belzutifan + lenvatinib in ccRCC

CHMP recommended combination therapy for advanced clear-cell RCC progressing after treatment that included a PD-(L)1 inhibitor. In previously released phase III comparisons against cabozantinib, PFS HR was 0.74 (95% CI 0.62–0.88) and ORR 53.1% versus 40.2%. Final OS did not cross the prespecified statistical boundary (HR 0.85; 95% CI 0.70–1.03).

RCC spatial transcriptomics and prostate DALTACs

A phase II sunitinib translational cohort explores tumour-microenvironment signatures (46 patients treated; 23 with spatial profiling). Separately, AR–p300/CBP DALTAC-1 offers a preclinical induced-proximity mechanism for AR-driven prostate cancer. Neither approach currently supports treatment selection.

Breast cancer
Original clinical follow-up / research letter

IDEA: late recurrence after radiotherapy omission

A JAMA Oncology research letter extends scrutiny of late local recurrence after breast-conserving surgery with endocrine therapy but without radiotherapy in selected younger postmenopausal ER-positive early breast cancer patients. It is nonrandomized; de-escalation must be individualized and aligned with current randomized evidence.

Translational Data / Basic Science

NER-deficiency signatures and cisplatin response

In TCGA breast cancer (n=943), 4.7% had evidence of NER deficiency without concurrent HRD; the ERCC2-derived score was associated with pathologic response in a cisplatin-treated TNBC cohort (n=50; P=0.0077), with additional analysis in TBCRC030. This remains investigational.

Cross-tumour, melanoma & rare tumours
Phase III Clinical Trials

INTerpath-001: individualized neoantigens plus pembrolizumab

NEJM phase III, 1,137 randomized patients after resection of stage IIB–IV cutaneous melanoma; 757 received personalized intismeran autogene plus pembrolizumab, and 380 placebo plus pembrolizumab. Recurrence-free survival was the primary endpoint. The platform has broad scientific implications, but tumour-type extrapolation is not justified.

Practice informing

NEJM original report

Phase I/II Clinical Trials

CAMORAL: perioperative chemoimmunotherapy in HNSCC

Randomized phase II, N=125. Two-year EFS was 77.8% versus 46.1%; HR 0.34 (95% CI 0.17–0.68; P=0.001). Grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in 79.4% versus 56.5%. Further phase III testing and comparisons with contemporary perioperative PD-1 approaches are needed.

Practice informing

JAMA Oncology original trial

Regulatory Issues

Pimicotinib: FDA approval in symptomatic TGCT

The FDA approved the CSF1R-pathway kinase inhibitor pimicotinib on 10 November for adults with symptomatic tenosynovial giant cell tumour when surgery could produce substantial functional morbidity. MANEUVER was a double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial; Week 25 RECIST ORR was the key efficacy measure.

CCR8-positive Tregs and resistance to radioimmunotherapy

Preclinical HNSCC work links intratumoral CCR8-positive regulatory T cells to resistance to RT plus anti-PD-1 and shows reversal by CCR8-depleting antibodies in experimental models.

What to watch next

European Commission final decisions and indication-specific SmPCs following CHMP recommendations; longer follow-up and biomarker subsets from TALAPRO-3 and LITESPARK-011; safety details and implementation after VOLGA; prospective validation of PARPi-resistance, NER, TROP2 and blood-based ovarian biomarkers.

Three take-home messages

1. VOLGA changes the US regulatory landscapeSurvival benefit supports the newly approved perioperative EV/durvalumab sequence for a clearly defined MIBC population.
2. GU and lung dominate this week's regulatory headlinesCHMP recommendations should not be misrepresented as final EU approval or Greek reimbursement.
3. Biomarkers and translational mechanisms are the research storyPromising studies on PARPi resistance, RNA detection, NER, TROP2 and AR-complex targeting need clinical validation.
