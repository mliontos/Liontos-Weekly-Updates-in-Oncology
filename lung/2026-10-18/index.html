FURTHER: firmonertinib in EGFR PACC-mutant NSCLC

Structurally guided treatment of uncommon EGFR P-loop/αC-helix compressing mutations. The phase Ib study reported durable antitumor activity, with median PFS of 11.1 months at 160 mg and 16.0 months at 240 mg daily.
