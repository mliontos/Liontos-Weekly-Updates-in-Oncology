Full Evidence Review · VS-7375

14 October 2026
Pancreatic cancer · KRAS G12D

TARGET-D 101 · Phase I/II

Evidence type: company-reported preliminary clinical data; not a peer-reviewed efficacy publication.

STATUS · INVESTIGATIONAL · No current change to standard of care

Study and population

VS-7375 is an investigational oral KRAS G12D ON/OFF inhibitor evaluated in TARGET-D 101 in advanced KRAS G12D-mutated solid tumors. The October update reported the 900 mg once-daily monotherapy cohort in metastatic PDAC after prior systemic therapy.

Efficacy population: 22 patients with 2L+ PDAC at 900 mg QD. The 2L subgroup included 8 patients.

Reported efficacy

41%CONFIRMED ORR · 2L+
50%CONFIRMED ORR · 2L
86%DCR · 2L+
67%6-MO PFS

The company also reported a 6-month OS landmark rate of 80% in the 2L+ cohort and two complete responses, one confirmed and one unconfirmed. Median PFS and OS were not mature in this early dataset.

Biomarker and safety observations

Early ctDNA dynamics were reported as pharmacodynamic evidence of target engagement. These molecular responses are hypothesis-generating and should not be interpreted as validated surrogates for PFS or OS.

Safety at the selected dose was described by the sponsor as favorable, but interpretation remains limited by cohort size, follow-up and the non-randomized design.

Critical appraisal

Strength: the magnitude of early tumor response is clinically notable in previously treated KRAS G12D-mutant PDAC.

Limitations: small cohort, single-arm design, immature time-to-event endpoints, selection inherent to early-phase trials, and sponsor-reported data. Cross-trial comparisons with chemotherapy or other KRAS-directed agents are not valid.

CLINICAL INTERPRETATION · Promising signal requiring prospective randomized confirmation.
