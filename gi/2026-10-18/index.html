Pancreatic Cancer

Practice impact: INVESTIGATIONAL

Colorectal Cancer

KRAS G12D + EGFR blockade

Early data support the biological rationale for combining KRAS G12D inhibition with anti-EGFR therapy in previously treated KRAS G12D-mutant mCRC, aiming to overcome EGFR-mediated adaptive feedback.

Practice impact: PROOF OF CONCEPT

ctDNA / Molecular Residual Disease

Interventional trials are evaluating ctDNA-positive, radiologically disease-free patients after curative-intent therapy. The clinically relevant question is whether MRD-guided intervention improves DFS and ultimately OS.

ctDNA clearance is not yet a validated survival surrogate.

Biliary Tract Cancer

FGFR2-directed strategies

Clinical development programs are exploring movement of FGFR2 inhibition into earlier treatment lines in FGFR2-positive advanced BTC. No change to first-line standard of care at present.

Gastric / GEJ Cancer

No new practice-changing mature randomized data this week. Key biomarkers for treatment selection remain HER2, CLDN18.2, MSI/MMR and PD-L1.

Esophageal · HCC · Anal Cancer

No new mature randomized dataset or regulatory decision with immediate impact on clinical practice during the reviewed week.

Biomarker Focus: KRAS G12D

Effective KRAS G12D inhibition could expand the proportion of PDAC patients with a clinically actionable molecular alteration.

Driver → Targetable alteration → Clinical activity → Randomized benefit → Practice change
