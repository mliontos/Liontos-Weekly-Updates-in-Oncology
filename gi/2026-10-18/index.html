MRD
Colorectal cancer

ctDNA / Molecular Residual Disease

Prospective interventional studies are evaluating patients who are ctDNA-positive but radiologically disease-free after curative-intent therapy.

Key methodological point
ctDNA clearance is biologically compelling, but it is not itself proof of improved DFS or OS.
CLINICAL USE · AWAIT RANDOMIZED OUTCOME DATA
