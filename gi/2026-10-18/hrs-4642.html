Full Evidence Review · HRS-4642 + adebrelimab

Cancer Cell · 12 October 2026
Pancreatic cancer · KRAS G12D

Chemotherapy-free Phase I/II study

Evidence type: peer-reviewed publication in Cancer Cell.

STATUS · INVESTIGATIONAL · Encouraging activity in refractory KRAS G12D-mutant PDAC

Population and design

Phase I/II trial (NCT06427239) of the selective KRAS G12D inhibitor HRS-4642 combined with PD-L1 blockade with adebrelimab in 48 previously treated patients with metastatic KRAS G12D-mutant PDAC.

The recommended Phase II dose cohort included 37 patients.

RP2D efficacy

43.2%CONFIRMED ORR
81.1%DCR
5.7mPFS · MO
11.5mOS · MO

95% CI: ORR 27.1–60.5%; DCR 64.8–92.0%; median PFS 4.0–7.5 months; median OS 9.2–16.9 months. Median duration of response was 6.9 months (95% CI 4.8–8.6).

Safety and biomarkers

No treatment-related deaths were reported. Hypercholesterolemia and anemia were the most common treatment-related adverse events in the published report. KRAS G12D variant allele frequency was explored as a potential marker of response and resistance.

Critical appraisal

The study provides peer-reviewed evidence that selective KRAS G12D inhibition can produce clinically meaningful tumor regression in refractory PDAC. However, it remains a small, non-randomized early-phase trial.

Because there is no HRS-4642-alone comparator, the incremental contribution of adebrelimab cannot be established. Randomized trials are required before the combination can influence standard treatment.
