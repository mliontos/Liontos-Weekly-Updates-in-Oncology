Breast cancer
Previously reported phase III · New methodological correspondence

NATALEE: six-year survival interpretation

The phase III NATALEE update showed six-year iDFS HR 0.741 and OS HR 0.809 (95% CI 0.661–0.989; nominal one-sided P=0.019), with 2.1 percentage points absolute six-year OS difference. This is a follow-up discussion of an existing trial, not a new pivotal readout this week.

Clinical interpretation: The six-year OS analysis was prespecified as descriptive, outside the multiplicity-controlled testing strategy, with no alpha allocated to this look. Repeated looks and multiple endpoints require family-wise type-I error control; nominal P=0.019 does not demonstrate formal confirmatory OS significance. Subsequent first-line CDK4/6 inhibitor use after distant relapse differed between arms (44.4% after control versus 21.0% after adjuvant ribociclib), warranting contextual interpretation rather than an assumption of bias. The iDFS benefit remains established.

Gynaecological & Genitourinary

Yousif et al. · Gynecologic Oncology · 2026
Observational · Bias-sensitive

GLP-1 receptor agonists and overall survival in endometrial cancer

Retrospective TriNetX cohort; 2,646 propensity-matched women per group. The diagnosis-anchored primary analysis showed OS HR 0.81 (95% CI 0.65–1.02; P=0.07), with 5-year OS 91.6% versus 90.2%. The striking association in a never-user comparison (HR approximately 0.56) attenuated substantially when exposure and control were defined without future information.

Critical appraisal: The control group comprised women with no recorded GLP-1RA/tirzepatide exposure on or before diagnosis; later initiation did not change baseline allocation. Defining controls as lifetime never-users can introduce survivor selection and immortal-time bias. Negative-control allergy events were more frequent among users, suggesting differential healthcare engagement. Stage was documented in fewer than 5%; histology and molecular class were unavailable. No causal anticancer benefit is established.

A review of genomic correlates of chemoradiotherapy response in cervical cancer, spanning candidate molecular signatures and resistance mechanisms.

Osorio et al. · Clinical Cancer Research · 17 November 2026
Phase I · Early

Intravesical CD40 agonist 2141-V11 in BCG-unresponsive NMIBC

Phase I intravesical immunotherapy in 25 patients. The reported recommended phase II dose was 70 mg; preliminary response was approximately 40%, with a reported response duration of 20 months. Translational signals include tertiary lymphoid structures and CXCL13-associated immune activity.

Critical appraisal: Small, early-phase, non-comparative dataset. Response estimates, durability and safety require confirmation; a complete adverse-event table was not independently verified for this draft.

Prostate Cancer and Prostatic Diseases · 19 November 2026
Phase Ib/II · Exploratory

ARC-6: etrumadenant combinations in mCRPC

Phase Ib/II program (N=173) investigating A2A/A2B adenosine receptor antagonism. In taxane-naïve subgroups, etrumadenant + zimberelimab + docetaxel (n=26) yielded composite ORR 55% and 6-month disease control 73%; docetaxel alone (n=25) had composite ORR 52% and 6-month disease control 33%.

Critical appraisal: Composite ORR is not synonymous with standard RECIST objective response. Small exploratory subgroups and non-definitive comparisons do not demonstrate survival benefit or establish a new standard.

Breast

Marra et al. · Clinical Cancer Research · 17 November 2026
Retrospective · Biomarker

CCNE1 amplification and therapeutic resistance

Retrospective molecular cohort of 6,318 breast cancers; 251 (4%) were CCNE1-amplified. Frequencies varied by subtype (HR+/HER2− 2.1%; HER2+ 6.7%; TNBC 8.7%). In HER2-positive patients receiving THP, median PFS was 8.4 versus 18 months (HR 2.33, 95% CI 1.31–4.12).

Critical appraisal: A compelling resistance-associated biomarker signal, but retrospective, subject to confounding and not a prospectively validated basis for selecting treatment.

De Laurentiis et al. · Annals of Oncology correspondence · 19 November 2026
Correspondence · Statistical appraisal

NATALEE: what does the six-year OS analysis establish?

The underlying six-year NATALEE report showed OS HR 0.809 (95% CI 0.661–0.989), nominal one-sided P=0.019; six-year OS 91.86% versus 89.73%. The sustained iDFS advantage was HR 0.741, with an absolute six-year difference of 5.3 percentage points.

Critical appraisal: The six-year OS analysis was prespecified as descriptive, outside the multiplicity-controlled testing strategy: no alpha was allocated to this OS look. Thus P=0.019 and a conventional 95% CI excluding 1 do not constitute formal confirmatory OS significance. Multiple endpoints and repeated looks require type-I error control. Subsequent CDK4/6 inhibitor use after distant relapse also differed (first post-relapse endocrine + CDK4/6i: 44.4% after NSAI alone versus 21.0% after adjuvant ribociclib); this complicates interpretation but is not proof of bias. The correspondence itself was not fully accessible, so its complete argument is not attributed here.

Oh et al. · npj Precision Oncology · 22 November 2026
Translational · Early

Spatial metastatic niches in lung adenocarcinoma

Spatial transcriptomics in eight matched primary tumour/lymph-node pairs identified candidate metastatic SEED niches associated with POSTN-positive stroma and CYP24A1 expression.

Critical appraisal: Hypothesis-generating spatial biology; very small paired sample and no established clinical test or treatment implication.

Cross-tumour & immunology

Ramsauer et al. · Nature · 18 November 2026
Preclinical · Mechanistic

Ferroptosis-induced PGE₂ drives immune escape

Preclinical work connects ferroptotic membrane damage and calcium signalling to p38 MAPK, COX-2/PGE₂ and EP2/EP4-mediated impairment of cDC1 and CD8 T-cell antitumour immunity. EP2/EP4 blockade restored immune activity in experimental models.

Critical appraisal: Mechanistically important and potentially actionable, but no clinical efficacy of this strategy is established in patients.
